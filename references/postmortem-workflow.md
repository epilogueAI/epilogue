# Post-Mortem & Narrative Epilogue Workflow

This document defines how to produce both a technical post-mortem and a narrative epilogue.

## Goal
Deliver two complementary outputs:
1. A clear, data-grounded Technical Post-Mortem.
2. A calm, respectful Narrative Epilogue that gives the story a proper ending.

## Phase 0 — Scope & Consent

1. Confirm what is being examined:
   - A specific token launch
   - An agent’s full history
   - A wallet / trading period
   - A failed or abandoned project

2. Ask whether the user wants:
   - Technical only
   - Narrative only
   - Both (recommended)

3. Note any sensitive framing the user prefers (e.g. “keep it neutral”, “be honest but not harsh”).

## Phase 1 — Data Collection

Collect only what is available through Bankr tools and public on-chain data:

- Deployment details (chain, date, deployer, initial parameters)
- Trading volume, liquidity history, holder count over time
- Creator fees earned (if applicable)
- Major price / volume events and timestamps
- Social or narrative signals only if they are already known or easily verifiable
- Any automations, skills, or strategies that were active

If data is incomplete, explicitly list what is missing. Never fabricate numbers or events.

## Phase 2 — Technical Post-Mortem

Structure:

1. **Identity**  
   What was examined (token / agent / period).

2. **Timeline of Key Events**  
   Chronological list of significant on-chain and observable events.

3. **Performance Summary**  
   Volume, fees, peak metrics, final state (factual only).

4. **What Worked**  
   Observable positive factors.

5. **What Did Not Work / Turning Points**  
   Observable negative factors or inflection points. Stay factual.

6. **Residual Value**  
   Any remaining fees, liquidity, or claims.

7. **Open Questions**  
   Anything that cannot be determined from available data.

Tone: precise, neutral, no blame language.

## Phase 3 — Narrative Epilogue

Write a short, self-contained piece (usually 300–700 words) that:

- Treats the subject with dignity
- Acknowledges both ambition and outcome
- Avoids sarcasm, mockery, or excessive drama
- Ends with a sense of closure rather than cliffhanger

Suggested internal structure (do not label these headings in the final text unless requested):
- Opening image or context
- The rise / intention
- The turning
- The present state
- Quiet closing

Style guidelines:
- Calm and slightly literary is preferred.
- First or third person is acceptable; match the user’s preference if stated.
- Never invent personal motives that are not supported by data or user input.

## Phase 4 — Delivery

1. Present Technical Post-Mortem and Narrative Epilogue as two clearly separated sections.
2. Offer to:
   - Save both as files
   - Generate a combined “Final Chapter” document
   - Mint a simple on-chain record or NFT of the epilogue (only if the user explicitly wants it)
3. End with a short, quiet closing line (e.g. “Chapter closed.”).

## Safety & Integrity Rules

- Do not speculate about the deployer’s or user’s private intentions.
- Do not claim causation that cannot be supported by the data.
- If the story is still unfolding, say so and offer a “Current Chapter” instead of a forced ending.
